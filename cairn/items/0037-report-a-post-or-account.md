## Proposal

A report control on posts and profiles with a short list of reasons.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reasons: spam, harassment, hate, illegal content, other
- [ ] One report per reporter per target; repeats are a no-op
- [ ] The reporter gets a confirmation; the reported user is not told who reported
