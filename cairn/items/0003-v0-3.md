## Ships

The site can be opened to strangers without becoming spam: rate limits, blocking, muting,
reporting, and an admin who can remove posts and suspend accounts.

## Done when

- [ ] A script posting in a loop is throttled within seconds
- [ ] A blocked user cannot see, reply to, or follow the blocker
- [ ] A reported post reaches an admin queue and can be removed from it
- [ ] A user can delete their account and everything they posted
- [ ] Terms and privacy policy are published

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- Automated content classification: later
- Appeals workflow: later
