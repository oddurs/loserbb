## Proposal

A repost puts someone else's post on your followers' home timelines, attributed
"reposted by @you". No quote posts in v1.0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] One repost per user per post; toggling undoes it
- [ ] A post reposted by two people you follow appears once, at its newest repost
- [ ] You cannot repost your own post
- [ ] Deleting the original removes every repost
